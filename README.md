# Spot-In — Room & People Finder

> **Find Your Space, Find Your People.**

## About

Spot-In is a web-based room finder designed to help students find available and less crowded spaces on campus.

Besides showing room occupancy, Spot-In also allows users to see who is currently in a room. This makes it easier to find a suitable place to study, work, or meet friends without having to check each room one by one.

Users can check in by scanning a QR code placed in the room. The system then verifies their location using GPS before creating an active session. The session is periodically checked to keep the room occupancy information up to date.

## Features

- User authentication and role-based access
- View available campus rooms
- View current room occupancy and capacity
- Find people currently in a room
- QR-based room check-in
- GPS location verification
- Active session management
- Session renewal notifications
- Manual check-out
- Room and user management for administrators

## How It Works

1. Log in to your Spot-In account.
2. Browse the available rooms and their current occupancy.
3. Choose a room based on its capacity and current number of users.
4. Go to the selected room.
5. Scan the QR code available in the room.
6. Allow GPS access for location verification.
7. If the location is valid, your session will be created.
8. The system will periodically ask whether you are still in the room.
9. End your session manually when you leave.

## Project Goal

Spot-In is built to make it easier for students to find a place to work or study and to find people on campus.

Instead of asking:

> "Where should I go?"

or

> "Where is my friend?"

Spot-In helps you find the answer.
